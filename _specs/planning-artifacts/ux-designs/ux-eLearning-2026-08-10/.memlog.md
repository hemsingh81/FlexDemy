- (decision) User reported amber 'not properly visible with blue(dark) color'. Root cause: BA5012 (darkened for white-text-on-button AA) only gets ~2.58:1 against navy #143358 when used as icon/text color directly on navy -- the original #EC7B38 had ~4.535:1 there and was fine, but fails white-text-on-button at ~2.82:1. No single hex clears AA in both roles (contrast-vs-white and contrast-vs-navy move in opposite directions as lightness changes, with no overlap at the 4.5:1 floor). Split into two tokens: citrus-amber (#BA5012) for on-light/button-fill roles, citrus-amber-on-dark (#EC7B38, the original value, given a permanent semantic role instead of being a deprecated marker) for icon/text placed directly on navy or a translucent overlay on navy.
