What is a combination reaction?

Unconfirmed

A combination reaction is one where two or more reactants join to form a single product. The general form is A + B → AB. You'll see this pattern again and again — it's the simplest of the four reaction types.

  • Iron and sulphur combine on heating to form iron sulphide
  • Quicklime reacts with water to form slaked lime, releasing heat
